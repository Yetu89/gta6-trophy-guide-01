Roadmap to the Platinum

Five stages, in order. Do the story first, then the stranger strands, then sweep the world for collectibles, then the activities and skill challenges, and finish with money and cleanup. The platinum — Vice Got a Price! — unlocks when the other 54 are done, and A Long Road (100% completion) is the last stop before it.

1 Story first

Play Chapters 1–4 and the final mission in order. Every chapter and named mission trophy here is unmissable story progress — just follow the mission markers. Keep a manual save at the start of each chapter as a precaution: sources conflict on whether missions can be replayed.

    2 Strangers & side strands

    Finish the stranger strands — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret — plus any 8 full strands for Strangers & Freaks. A strand only counts when its final mission is done. Strands stay available after the story.

      3 Collectibles & exploration

      Sweep Leonida: cards, WAiNK spots, vehicles, meditation spots and all 52 areas. These are completion counters — collect as you travel instead of leaving one giant sweep for the end, and tick each trophy off here as its counter fills.

        4 Activities & skill challenges

        Stunts, races, the shooting range, fishing, the arcade, robberies and the one-shot conditions. This is where the missable flags live — read each trophy's “when it can be missed” note before you attempt it, and save manually first.

          5 Money & cleanup

          Launder $250,000 in marked bills, spend $100,000 in shops, buy all 8 garages, register 10 vehicles — then check the in-game 100% stat for A Long Road. When it pops, the platinum follows.

            About this guide — read this first

            What this guide is: a checklist and roadmap for the 55 official PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned). Every name, description and grade is shown exactly as PlayStation publishes it. Each trophy's guide is written in our own words for this entry and names its source beside it.

            Where the list comes from (verified 19 Nov 2026): the official PlayStation list as mirrored by Exophase and PSNProfiles, cross-checked on the PlanetGTA6 trophy page, the OutpuTLag trophy list and the GTAViceCityHub achievements list. The full list — all 55 names, descriptions and grades — agrees across those sources. Source names and addresses are shown beside each trophy's guide as plain text you can copy.

            Honesty note: GTA VI launched on 19 Nov 2026 — the day this guide was built. Where a step is inferred from the official trophy description rather than a verified location, the guide says so (“from the official description…”). This guide invents no map coordinates, no NPC schedules and no precise locations it cannot verify. Counts the game itself tracks (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 Top Fun locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas of Leonida, 9 gangs, 8 garage properties) are given as PlayStation tracks them.

            Community-reported locations — UNVERIFIED: some trophies now list specific places (meditation spots, shooting ranges, bank vaults, garages, gang operations, jewelry stores, fences, card leads) gathered from launch-day community guides — Beebom, PowerPyx, GTABase, GTADB, KeenGamer, HowToVideoGame, GTAVibes, CryptoGames, PlayDay.One and ConsolePCGaming. Every one of those entries is labelled UNVERIFIED in the guide and names its source, because the game has only just launched, Rockstar has published no official map, and early guides already disagree with each other (for example on how the 18 meditations are counted). Treat each listed place as a lead to check in your own game, not a confirmed fact — and where no credible source names a location, this guide lists none rather than inventing one.

            Progress counters: trophies with a known target (250 stunts, 18 meditation spots, $250,000 laundered, and the rest) have − / + counters on their cards. Counters save in this browser separately from your earned ticks. Hitting a counter's target ticks that trophy as earned automatically; dropping back below the target removes that automatic tick (a tick you made yourself by hand is never removed by a counter). Counters stay visible in spoiler-safe mode, since a bare “0/18 spots” reveals no story detail.

            Missable flags: only what the official description itself implies — a “without being detected” condition, a back-to-back streak “without losing your Wanted Level”, “in a single use of Combat Focus”, “at every spot / location” completion counters, and “escape” conditions. Story chapter and mission trophies are unmissable story progress.

            Your data stays yours: ticks are saved only in this browser (localStorage). Export downloads a JSON file; import reads it back. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and this page makes no network requests at all.
